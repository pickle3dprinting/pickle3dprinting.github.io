About

This is our home 3D printing studio. We design pieces, slice them, and print them layer by layer. Every print here started as an idea and a spool of filament. Proudly operating Bambu Lab printers since 2025.

  • Printer: Bambu Lab
  • Filament: Bambu Lab PLA Basic, PLA Matte, and PLA Wood
